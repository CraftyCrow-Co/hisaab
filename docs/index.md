# Hisaab

*Know what you have and what's coming, across every business and person you handle,
in one place.*

Hisaab is one Google Sheet. You write money down as it moves — in, out, expected —
and it answers the only two questions that actually cause money stress:

**Do I have enough right now? Is enough coming in on time?**

₹112, once. It copies into your own Google Drive and stays there. No account, no app,
no server of ours between you and your numbers — which is also why there's nothing to
renew.

---

## Finance, not accounting

The difference matters more than it sounds.

Accounting looks backwards and has to be exact, because someone else is checking:
your CA, the tax department. Finance looks forwards and only has to be honest,
because you're the one deciding.

Hisaab is the second kind. It won't file your returns. It'll tell you whether you can
afford the thing you're about to say yes to.

---

## How a transaction is recorded

Every movement of money is **one row**, and four columns carry the whole thing:

```
Date        when it moved
Amount      signed — money in is positive, money out is negative
From        where it came from
To          where it went
```

Everything else is optional and layered on top:

- **Purpose** — exactly one of seven, and the only column that adds to 100%:
  `Earned` · `Specific cost` · `Running cost` · `Invested` · `Loan` · `Took home` ·
  `Self`
- **Count Under** — free words that group rows (a job, a client, a project). Several
  words per row are lenses on the same rupee, not slices of it, so each counts the
  full amount and tag totals can legitimately exceed the whole.
- **Mode** — only *how* the money moved. Bank, Cash, UPI, Card, Credit.
- **Is Planned / Planned on** — money expected but not yet moved. Planned rows never
  touch the balance.

Names are flat and lowercase, with a slash for a family: `bank/idfc`,
`staff/ramesh`, `client/acme`. Typing `staff` totals everyone you pay. You never set
up a category list — your typing builds the hierarchy.

---

## The lesson built into it

A minus is not automatically a cost.

`Running cost` and `Specific cost` make you poorer. `Invested`, `Loan` and
`Took home` are money that **moved** — into an asset, against a debt, or into your
life. Confusing the two is why a profitable business feels broke, and separating them
is the whole point of the Purpose column.

---

## What it is not

Not a replacement for your CA. Not a GST filing tool. Not a mobile app, not a cloud
service, not trying to be QuickBooks or Tally.

It's a sharp personal tool — minimal, reliable, out of the way.
