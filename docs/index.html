Questions

Is this accounting software?

No — and the difference matters more than it sounds. Accounting looks backwards and has to be exact, because someone else is checking: your CA, the tax department.
Finance looks forwards and only has to be honest, because you're the one deciding.
Hisaab is the second kind. It won't file your returns. It'll tell you whether you can afford the thing you're about to say yes to.

Is my data private?

It's your own Google Sheet, in your Drive. We never see it.

Couldn't I just build this myself?

Try it. We did for years. Every version ended half-built and redesigned, with our time going into the sheet instead of the money.
We got it down to 4 columns: date, amount, from, to. Can't get simpler, and that simplicity took years to find. We did it once, so you don't have to.
Your focus stays on your money, not the sheet.

Do I need to be good with spreadsheets?

Nope. You just type what came in and what went out. Hisaab does the rest. You can do more as you get comfortable with it.

Is it for one person, a family, or a business?

All of them. One person, a family, or many businesses — all in the same sheet.

हिसाब · حساب

From the Arabic ḥisāb meaning "to count or to reckon."
Over a thousand years ago, Arab mathematicians used it for ḥisāb al-hind.
It refers to the Indian art of counting that we use all over the world today.
The word has always meant one quiet thing:
knowing where you stand.
