# What's new in Hisaab

*Every change below came from using Hisaab ourselves, or from something you told us. Keep it coming. — Jeet*

## 30 Sep 2026

- **Less to take in on day one.** Your copy opens with five tabs. Tick the others on from Start Here when you want them, even on your phone.  
  *Hidden tabs keep calculating in the background, so nothing is lost while a tab is out of sight.*
- **A guide you can read in one go.** Plain words, and every screen it describes now matches your sheet.  
  *We read every line against the live sheet and rewrote whatever had fallen behind it.*
- **A clean start.** A new copy opens with no stray dates or error cells.  
  *Empty rows were carrying leftover codes that made formulas think they held data. We cleared them.*
- **Your profit report ignores starting balances.** The money you began with is no longer flagged as untagged income.
- **Your AI helper gets more accurate with time.** Turning a bank statement into rows, it labels each payee the way you did before.  
  *The prompt first checks a short list of how you've named payees, then your own Log.*
- **Your net worth is right.** Investments you owned before you started now count for you, not against you.  
  *Money you already had on day one, like an FD or a fund, was being subtracted instead of added.*
- **Budgets judge the right stretch of time.** A weekly limit checks this week, not everything since you set it.  
  *Setting a start date quietly turned "this week" into "since the start date". Now the start date only narrows the window.*
- **Client share counts real income only.** Loans, your own top-ups and starting balances no longer inflate who pays you most.  
  *Every incoming amount was being counted as income. Now only rows marked Earned count.*
- **See your next 12 months of commitments,** split by what each payment is for.  
  *The report now starts from this month and runs twelve months ahead, one column per Purpose.*
